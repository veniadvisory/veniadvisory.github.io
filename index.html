    Why now

    Roles change fast

    New tools and processes outpace annual training plans. Companies need to know what skills they have today.

    CVs are less reliable

    AI-written applications make CVs harder to judge. Seeing someone do the work is more reliable.

    AI lowers the cost

    Marking and personal plans no longer need an expert at every step, so assessment can cover everyone.

    Start with one team.

    Run a pilot with one team or one open role. You’ll see real skill gaps before rolling out further.
